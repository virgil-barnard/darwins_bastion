EXPEDITION 01

Ignite your core.

Clear three raid fronts. Command Mote to recover salvage, evolve your core, and choose what to bring into the next sector.

Shape what survives.

Your core fires automatically. Spend energy on mutations in three endlessly growing branches. Each purchase reveals two new possibilities.

◇ Collectors bring crystals to their nests. △ Raiders telegraph a siphon beam, steal energy, and retreat with visible cargo. ⬡ Scavengers feed on wreckage; some latch onto your core to consume biomass. Colonies use successful deliveries to reproduce.

All three species inherit named lineages: skimmers dodge, carapaces resist kinetic fire, and renewals regrow. Recover three pink gene traces from a lineage to reveal two player grafts. Choose one in Living research; it joins your skill constellation.

Click to command your companions; an organism click also inspects its ancestry. Press R to recall your crew. Mote harvests; Sentinel can be recruited at a checkpoint. Use Space to charge a resonance wave. It interrupts siphons, knocks organisms away, and reclaims cargo. Time slows during the wave so you can see the result. Open the tree to think at 0.15× speed; collapse it to watch your build unfold.

Each sector contains three raid fronts. Clear the last front, including its larger anchor, to choose a free reward and move to a new environment. Fronts arrive 45 seconds apart, with an eight-second warning. Their pressure grows with elapsed time. Later mutations cost both energy and biomass. Seasons change every 75 seconds. Enemy genomes are selected every 40 seconds from measured survival and completed deliveries. Survive as long as you can.
